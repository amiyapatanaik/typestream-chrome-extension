Privacy Policy

Effective June 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Typestream Voice, the open-source Chrome extension ("the Extension"), handles your information. The Extension is powered by the Typestream speech-to-text API and is maintained as part of the Typestream project. Typestream is a brand owned and operated by Small Experiments LLP (UEN T26LL0245C), registered in Singapore.

The Extension is designed with privacy as a core principle: your API key and dictation history stay on your device, and audio sent for transcription is handled under Typestream's Zero Data Retention policy.

Our core privacy philosophy: Your voice and transcripts belong to you. The Extension does not sell your data, run advertising trackers, or collect analytics about your browsing activity.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Extension stores the following data in Chrome local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly send audio for transcription (see Section 3).

A. Information You Provide

  • Typestream API key: Required to authenticate transcription requests. Stored only on your device.
  • Extension preferences: Settings such as whether to save dictation history locally, auto-copy to clipboard, and play notification sounds.
  • Keyboard shortcut preference: Your chosen dictation hotkey configuration.

B. Information Generated by the Extension

  • Dictation history (optional): If enabled in settings, recent transcripts and timestamps are saved locally in the Extension popup for your convenience. You can delete individual entries or clear all local data at any time from the settings page.

You can remove all locally stored Extension data at any time using the Clear All Local Data button in Extension settings.

3. Audio and Transcription Processing

When you trigger dictation, the Extension accesses your microphone through a temporary offscreen document (a Chrome requirement for Manifest V3 extensions). Audio is recorded only while dictation is active and is sent over encrypted HTTPS to api.typestream.dev for transcription.

  • No storage of audio or transcripts on Typestream servers: Typestream processes audio in memory, returns the transcript to the Extension, and immediately deletes both the audio and transcript from its servers once the API response is sent.
  • No AI training: Your audio and transcripts are not used to train machine learning models.
  • Subprocessor retention: Enterprise subprocessors used by Typestream may temporarily retain audio or transcript data for up to 30 days strictly for legal compliance, abuse prevention, and trust & safety. After that period, the data is permanently deleted. See the Typestream Privacy Policy for full details.

API requests include your API key for authentication and standard request metadata (such as timestamps and audio duration) for billing and performance monitoring. This metadata does not include the contents of your audio or transcripts.

4. Permissions and How They Are Used

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions solely to provide dictation functionality:

  • Microphone: Captures your voice while dictation is active.
  • Storage: Saves your API key, preferences, and optional local history.
  • Clipboard write: Copies transcribed text when no text field is focused, if auto-copy is enabled.
  • Active tab & scripting: Inserts transcribed text at your cursor position on the current page.
  • Offscreen document: Required by Chrome to access the microphone from a Manifest V3 service worker.
  • Host permission (api.typestream.dev): Sends audio for transcription and receives results.
  • Global keyboard shortcut: Lets you start and stop dictation from anywhere in the browser.

The Extension injects a content script into the active tab only when you invoke it (for example, by pressing your dictation keyboard shortcut or opening the extension popup). The script displays the dictation overlay and inserts text at your cursor. We do not read, collect, or transmit the contents of web pages you visit. The content script only interacts with the active text field when inserting your dictated text.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not operate analytics, advertising, or tracking services within the Extension.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, page content, or form data from websites you visit.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • The Extension developers do not receive your API key, audio, or transcripts.

6. Third-Party Services

Transcription is provided by the Typestream API. When you use dictation, your audio is processed according to the Typestream Privacy Policy, including account, billing, and API metadata practices for your Typestream developer account.

Links in the Extension to typestream.dev (for example, to obtain an API key) are governed by Typestream's website privacy practices.

7. Data Security

All communication between the Extension and the Typestream API uses TLS/HTTPS encryption. Your API key is stored in Chrome's extension-local storage, which is isolated from ordinary websites. You are responsible for keeping your API key confidential and revoking it from the Typestream dashboard if your device is compromised.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Disable local dictation history in Extension settings.
  • Delete individual history entries from the Extension popup.
  • Clear all Extension data (API key, history, and settings) from the settings page.
  • Revoke microphone access through Chrome's site/extension permission controls.
  • Remove the Extension entirely to delete all locally stored data.

For rights related to your Typestream developer account, API usage, or billing data, contact us using the details in Section 10 or refer to the Typestream Privacy Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Extension or applicable legal requirements. Material changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the Extension after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
